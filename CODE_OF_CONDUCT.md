# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, practical community around private, secure developer
tooling. Participate in good faith, assume reasonable intent, welcome differing
experience levels, and keep feedback focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, or the publication of another
person's private logs, credentials, source code, or usage data are not
acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate this
standard.

For conduct concerns, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub rather than
opening a public issue with sensitive details.
